Privacy Policy

ShortClipEnjoyer (the "App"), a personal desktop tool. Last updated: November 16, 2026.

What the App does

The App runs on the user's own Windows computer. It turns clips from video streams into short vertical videos and, when the user chooses, uploads them to the user's own TikTok, YouTube and Instagram accounts through each platform's official APIs.

Information we access

TikTok

  • Account information: your display name (scope user.info.basic), used only to show which account is connected.
  • Video posting: the App uploads videos you choose to your account (scopes video.upload and video.publish) and reads the status of those uploads.

YouTube (Google)

  • Video uploads: the App uploads videos you choose to your YouTube channel (scope youtube.upload).
  • Channel and video data: the App reads your channel name and the view, like and comment counts of videos it uploaded (scope youtube.readonly), and shows them to you in the App.

Instagram (Meta)

  • The App publishes Reels you choose to your Instagram account and reads their view, like and comment counts.

For every platform, the App also stores the access tokens the platform gives it after you sign in.

How information is stored

All data, including access tokens, is stored only on your own computer, encrypted with Windows DPAPI. The App has no servers. No data is sent to the developer or anyone else, except the requests the App makes directly to TikTok, Google and Meta on your behalf.

Google API Services

The App's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is used only to provide the features described above, is never transferred to others, and is never used for advertising. By using the YouTube features you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share your information with anyone. We do not use it for advertising or analytics.

Your choices

You can disconnect your account in the App at any time. You can also revoke the App's access at any time: in TikTok under Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions; for Google at myaccount.google.com/permissions; for Instagram under Settings → Apps and websites. Deleting the App's data folder removes all stored information.

Children

The App is not intended for children under 13.
