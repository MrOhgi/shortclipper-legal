Privacy Policy

ShortClipEnjoyer (the "App"), a personal desktop tool. Last updated: November 16, 2026.

What the App does

The App runs on the user's own Windows computer. It turns clips from video streams into short vertical videos and, when the user chooses, uploads them to the user's own TikTok account through TikTok's official APIs.

Information we access

  • TikTok account information: your display name and basic profile info (scope user.info.basic), used only to show which account is connected.
  • Video posting: the App uploads videos you choose to your account (scopes video.upload and video.publish) and reads the status of those uploads.
  • Access tokens: the tokens TikTok gives the App after you sign in.

How information is stored

All data, including access tokens, is stored only on your own computer, encrypted with Windows DPAPI. The App has no servers. No data is sent to the developer or anyone else, except the requests the App makes directly to TikTok on your behalf.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share your information with anyone. We do not use it for advertising or analytics.

Your choices

You can disconnect your account in the App at any time. You can also revoke the App's access in TikTok under Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions. Deleting the App's data folder removes all stored information.

Children

The App is not intended for children under 13.
